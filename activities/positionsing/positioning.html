What is normal flow in HTML?

Normal flow is the default way that HTML elements are laid out on a webpage.

What does float do? Why do you need to clear the float?

Float allows you to position an element to the left or right of its container, allowing other content to wrap around it. Clearing the float is to prevent elements from wrapping around the floated element so that the layout remains consistent and elements are properly aligned.

What do absolute, fixed, and relative do?

Absolute positioning removes an element from the normal flow and positions it nearest positioned. Fixed positioning removes an element from the normal flow and positions it relative to the viewport. Relative positioning moves it to its normal position in the document flow.
