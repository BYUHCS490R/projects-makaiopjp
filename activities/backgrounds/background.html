Background Images

When should you use a background image, and when should you use the IMG tag?

  • Use a background image when the image is purely decorative and does not add meaningful content to the page. Background images are typically used for design purposes, such as adding texture, color, or visual interest to a section of a webpage.
  • Use the IMG tag when the image is essential to the content and provides information or context that cannot be conveyed through text alone. The IMG tag is used for images that are part of the content, such as product photos, infographics, or any image that contributes to the understanding of the page's message.
