MMA & Why it interests me

MMA (Mixed Martial Arts) is a full-contact combat sport that allows fighters to combine techniques that they have learned or mastered in a Martial Arts Match. Various martial arts are used to including boxing, wrestling, judo, karate, Brazilian jiu-jitsu and the list goes on. MMA is not just fighting, it is a complete sport that requires physical and mental strength of each opponent. it is a sport that demands dedication, discipline, and respect for the art.

Ever since I was a child, I have been fascinated by the Martial Arts. My dad taught me the basics of Karate. I did judo during the duration of my high school years. The combination of different martial arts and the strategic nature of the sport has always intrigued me. The discipline and dedication required to become a successful MMA fighter is something I deeply admire.
