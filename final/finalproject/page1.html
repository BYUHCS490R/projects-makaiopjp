History of the Denver Broncos

The Denver Broncos are a professional American football team based in Denver, Colorado. They were founded in 1960 as a charter member of the American Football League (AFL) and joined the National Football League (NFL) in 1970 when the AFL-NFL merger occurred.

Throughout their history, the Broncos have been known for their passionate fan base and competitive spirit. They have won three Super Bowl championships (1997, 1998, and 2015) and have made numerous playoff appearances. The team has had several legendary players, including John Elway, Terrell Davis, and Peyton Manning.

The Broncos play their home games at Empower Field at Mile High, which is located in Denver. The team's colors are orange and blue, and their mascot is a horse named "Miles." The Broncos have a rich history of success and are considered one of the most iconic franchises in the NFL.

Key Eras and Championships

Over the decades, the Broncos have built several eras that still stand out. The John Elway years remain one of the most memorable stretches, delivering consistent playoff appearances and some of the most iconic moments in team history. Later, under head coach Mike Shanahan, the Broncos reached new heights by winning back to back Super Bowls in 1997 and 1998 accomplishments that remain a point of pride among fans.The franchise returned to championship form in 2016, winning Super Bowl 50 with a dominant defense and strong leadership across the roster. It was a reminder of how the Broncos, even after rebuilding phases, could rise again and compete at the highest level. Another impressive run came between 2011 and 2014, when the Broncos reached four straight AFC Championship Games. Led by Peyton Manning and surrounded by a talented roster, the team showed a level of consistency and competitiveness that few franchises can claim. More recent roster moves including the acquisition of quarterback Drew Lock in 2020 signaled the Broncos continuing effort to reload and shape their future. As of today the team has won 15 division titles, 8 conference championships, and 3 Super Bowl titles, making them one of the most successful franchises in NFL history.

Stadium Timeline

Home field identity has always been a major part of the Broncos story. The evolution of their stadium reflects the team's growth and different eras of the franchise. Stadium Timeline

  • 1960-2000 Mile High Stadium
  • 2000-2011 Invesco Field at Mile High
  • 2011-2016 Sports Authority Field at Mile High
  • 2016-2019 Broncos Stadium at Mile High
  • 2019-present Empower Field at Mile High

Each name change came with renovations new sponsorships, and fresh memories for fans. No matter what the stadium was called, the atmosphere remained electric.

Owner History

Ownership has changed several times throughout the team's history, with each owner contributing to the franchise's development and success. Owner History

  • 1960-1961 Bob Howsam
  • 1961-1981 Gerald Phipps
  • 1981-1984 Edgar Kaiser Jr.
  • 1984-1998 Pat Bowlen
  • 1998-2024 Pat Bowlen (Trust)
  • 2024-present Walton-Penner Family Ownership Group

The transitions mattered because the ownership dicisions affect evefything from front office hires to stadium improvements and community engagement. showing how each owner evolved the franchise under different leadership, and explains why the broncos are where they are today.
