History of the Denver Broncos

The Denver Broncos are a professional American football team based in Denver, Colorado. They were founded in 1960 as a charter member of the American Football League (AFL) and joined the National Football League (NFL) in 1970 when the AFL-NFL merger occurred.

Throughout their history, the Broncos have been known for their passionate fan base and competitive spirit. They have won three Super Bowl championships (1997, 1998, and 2015) and have made numerous playoff appearances. The team has had several legendary players, including John Elway, Terrell Davis, and Peyton Manning.

The Broncos play their home games at Empower Field at Mile High, which is located in Denver. The team's colors are orange and blue, and their mascot is a horse named "Miles." The Broncos have a rich history of success and are considered one of the most iconic franchises in the NFL.
